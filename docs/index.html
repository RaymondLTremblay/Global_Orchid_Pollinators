Global Orchid Pollinators

Mapping the global distribution of orchid pollinators. Pollination records are compiled from the published literature into a single workbook, then georeferenced species by species and plotted.

Georeferencing: Natalia Palou, Caleb Pacheco & Naan Lamoso · University of Puerto Rico

Distribution maps

Every georeferenced record on one map, plus a map per subfamily. Click any point for the species, locality, traits and named pollinators.

Source data

The pollination records are drawn from the global orchid pollination database compiled by James D. Ackerman and colleagues, archived on Zenodo under doi:10.5281/zenodo.7263689 and published as Ackerman et al. (2023), Beyond the various contrivances by which orchids are pollinated: global patterns in orchid pollination biology, Botanical Journal of the Linnean Society 202: 295–324. Released under CC BY 4.0.

The work shown here adds georeferencing to that database, species by species, so the records can be mapped. Coordinates were compiled from the primary literature by Natalia Palou, Caleb Pacheco and Naan Lamoso.
