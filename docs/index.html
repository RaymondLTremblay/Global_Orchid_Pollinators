Global Orchid Pollinators

Mapping the global distribution of orchid pollinators. Pollination records are compiled from the published literature into a single workbook, then georeferenced species by species and plotted.

Natalia Palou & Caleb Pacheco · University of Puerto Rico

Distribution maps

Every georeferenced record on one map, plus a map per subfamily. Click any point for the species, locality, traits and named pollinators.

Interactive explorer

Filter by subfamily and drill down through the pollinators — group, then family, then genus — with the map redrawing as you go.

Georeferencing is ongoing, Americas first and Asia to follow, so most of the workbook has no coordinates yet. That is the current state of the work, not a gap in the underlying records.
